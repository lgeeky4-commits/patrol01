# Map Time Lens update

The top-right time slider is a visual filter for marks and reports in the currently visible room. Move the slider left to focus on older marks or right to focus on newer marks. Marks close to the selected time glow brighter in full color. Other marks stay clickable but become more transparent and gray.

The app never removes marks when sliding; mission confirmation and original permissions remain unchanged. Press NEWEST to jump to the newest mark.

Small reports are now visible at default 100% map zoom as muted gray dots with 80% opacity. Zooming to 170% or more brings back the close-time color/glow effect.

No timestamp information is sent to an external service by this feature. The slider is local to your device. Real camera/voice features still require permissions.
